Why I do this

I started taking photos obsessively after my twins were born in 2014. The days were so hard, but at the end of the day or a week, I had these moments of joy and beauty that made the exhaustion and stress melt away.

Then, as a camp director, I did the same thing. My photos were for me. Being a camp director is stressful and often lonely. Having images of the joy on the faces and the beauty of the spaces reminded me why I did that work. Sharing them inspired pride, brought joy to my community.

I love doing camps because I can give these images and the joy of sharing them to people whose place I know so well.
