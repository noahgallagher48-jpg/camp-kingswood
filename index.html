The finer schedule takes its shape on arrival, around the camp's own rhythm.

What lands afterward

  • The library. A finished, organized set of no fewer than 30 frames, delivered by August 23, licensed for Kingswood's marketing, development, and community use.
  • The summer media library, closed and organized. The camp's own media from the summer, organized into a clean archive and handed back by August 30 with written instructions for maintaining it.
  • Two photo books. A general donor book and one dedicated book, both scoped on site during the weekend. Layouts come to Kingswood for approval.
  • Prints. A print store of selected frames for the Kingswood community follows; details settle after the weekend.

The media team

Your photographers shoot alongside me the whole weekend, and I am there in support of their success. Settings, seeing, and a debrief each day. They keep shooting after I leave; the weekend is built to make their next month better, not to replace them.

The walk

One thing the weekend needs from camp: a walk around the grounds with people who carry a long attachment to Kingswood. Alumni, children of alumni, staff who grew up here. Hearing what this place means to the people who love it is where the photographs come from. Naming the walking companions before Friday helps.

How this page works

Questions and notes between now and the weekend go to noah@abba-photo.com. They get answered, and anything that changes the plan is folded into this page, so the link you have is always current.
